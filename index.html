About Me

Hey there! I'm Matthew Skiles. I've been an icon and visual designer for 15+ years, creating hand-crafted artistic expressions for developers big and small.

When I'm not on my desk and in front of a computer, I draw art on paper and canvasses. There is something humanizing about being able to touch something I’ve created and sharing it and its story to the people who matter the most to me. I also love spending time with my wife and kids every chance I get. These moments that I have with them remind me that something mundane, such as going to the grocery store or doing house chores, can turn into a great big adventure.

At the end of the day, this is my goal, too, for my designs. To treat every project as one big adventure. To take my clients on a great ride of making fun and pretty things and for them to be proud of the visuals of their product because it builds a unique connection with their users. So, if you are passionate about showing life and art through your app, let's make something beautiful.
